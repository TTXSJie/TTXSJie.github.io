Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-09-28

These tools are used by one person, the owner, with the owner's own Google account. They are not offered to anyone else.

Data accessed

Through Google APIs, the tools access data in the owner's own account only: files in Google Drive, email messages in Gmail, and items in Google Tasks.

How the data is used

  • Google Drive files are synced between Google Drive and computers controlled by the owner.
  • Some email messages are read to produce summaries for the owner. To do this, their text is processed by an AI text service the owner has chosen.
  • Google Tasks items are created and updated as reminders for the owner.

Storage and sharing

Copies of the data are kept only on computers controlled by the owner. No data is sold, used for advertising, or shared with anyone for any other purpose.

This website uses no cookies, analytics, or tracking.

Google API Services User Data Policy

Use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time from Google Account → Third-party connections.
